About

A small, free study site.

Hole Learning Hub offers short lessons and self-checking practice quizzes in three subjects: math, geography, and language arts. The material is aimed at middle-school level review, but anyone is welcome to use it.

What you will find

  • Lessons with worked examples you can follow step by step.
  • Practice quizzes that explain every answer, right or wrong.
  • No accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Quiz scores are not saved or sent anywhere.

Lessons by grade

The grade pages (Pre-K through 12th) are organized by topic area and follow the Georgia Standards of Excellence, the state standards for Georgia public schools. This site is not run by or affiliated with Coffee County Schools or any school district.

A note on accuracy

The content is written as a study aid and checked for mistakes, but it is not a substitute for your textbook or teacher. If your class uses a different method or notation, follow your class.
