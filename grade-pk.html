Pre-K topics follow the spirit of Georgia's early learning standards for four-year-olds. A grown-up can read these lessons aloud and do the Try it parts together. This is an unofficial study aid, not a Coffee County Schools resource.

Math

Number Sense and Counting

Counting objects to 10

Touch each object once as you say a number word. The last number you say tells how many there are.

Try it: Count the stars: ★ ★ ★ ★

Show answer

4. Touch each star and say 1, 2, 3, 4.

More, fewer, and the same

To compare two groups, line the objects up in pairs. The group with objects left over has more.

Try it: There are 3 cups and 5 plates. Which group has more?

Show answer

The plates. After pairing 3 cups with 3 plates, 2 plates are left over.

Shapes and Patterns

Naming shapes

A circle is round. A square has 4 sides that are the same length. A triangle has 3 sides.

Try it: What shape has 3 sides?

Show answer

A triangle.

Making patterns

A pattern repeats in the same order. Look for the part that repeats, then say what comes next.

Try it: Red, blue, red, blue, __. What comes next?

Show answer

Red.

Geography

My Community

Where I live and learn

You live in a home and learn at school. Homes, schools, and stores are all part of a community.

Try it: Name one place in your community where you can borrow books.

Show answer

A library.

Community helpers

Community helpers do jobs that keep us safe and healthy. Firefighters, doctors, and teachers are helpers.

Try it: Who helps put out a fire?

Show answer

A firefighter.

Where Things Are

Position words

Words like near, far, over, under, in front of, and behind tell where something is.

Try it: A cat sits under the table. Is the cat over the table or under it?

Show answer

Under the table.

A map is a picture of a place

A map shows a place as if you were looking down from above. It helps us find where things are.

Try it: What does a map help you find?

Show answer

Where places and things are.

Language Arts

Letters and Sounds

Letter names and first sounds

Every letter has a name and a sound. The first sound in ball is /b/, which is the letter B.

Try it: What letter makes the first sound in sun?

Show answer

S.

Rhyming words

Rhyming words end with the same sound, like cat and hat.

Try it: Which word rhymes with dog: log or cup?

Show answer

Log.

Stories and Listening

Retelling a story

Tell what happens at the beginning, the middle, and the end of a story, in order.

Try it: Think of a story you know. What happens first?

Show answer

Answers will vary. Say the first event before the middle and the end.

Characters and setting

The characters are who the story is about. The setting is where and when it happens.

Try it: In a story about a bear in a cave, who is the character and what is the setting?

Show answer

The bear is the character. The cave is the setting.
