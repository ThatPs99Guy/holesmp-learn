Math

Number and Operations in Base Ten

Place value to 1,000

A three-digit number has hundreds, tens, and ones. 463 is 4 hundreds, 6 tens, and 3 ones.

Try it: How many tens are in 785?

Show answer

8 tens.

Adding and subtracting within 1,000

Line up the place values. Add or subtract ones, then tens, then hundreds. Regroup when needed.

Try it: What is 458 − 120?

Show answer

338.

Measurement and Data

Measuring length

Line up the zero mark of the ruler with one end of the object. 12 inches make 1 foot.

Try it: How many inches are in 2 feet?

Show answer

24 inches.

Telling time

The short hand shows the hour and the long hand shows the minutes. Each number on the clock stands for 5 minutes.

Try it: The hour hand is just past 3 and the minute hand points at the 3. What time is it?

Show answer

3:15.

Geography

World Geography Basics

Continents and oceans

There are 7 continents and 5 oceans. The Pacific is the largest ocean.

Try it: Name the largest ocean.

Show answer

The Pacific Ocean.

Finding places on a grid

A grid map uses letters and numbers to name each square, like B3. Find the column letter, then the row number.

Try it: In the grid name B3, which part is the column?

Show answer

The letter B.

Our Home: Georgia

Georgia's regions

Georgia has five regions: Blue Ridge Mountains, Ridge and Valley, Appalachian Plateau, Piedmont, and Coastal Plain.

Try it: Douglas, in Coffee County, is in which region?

Show answer

The Coastal Plain, in south Georgia.

Rivers of Georgia

Rivers carry water, help people travel, and give animals homes. The Savannah River forms part of Georgia's eastern border.

Try it: Name one river in Georgia.

Show answer

The Savannah River, the Chattahoochee River, or another Georgia river.

Language Arts

Reading Informational Text

Main topic and key details

The main topic is what a text is mostly about. Key details are facts that tell more about it.

Try it: A passage describes how bees make honey. What is the main topic?

Show answer

How bees make honey.

Text features

Headings, captions, bold words, and a glossary help you find information fast.

Try it: Which text feature explains what a picture shows?

Show answer

The caption.

Language

Plural nouns

Add -s to most nouns. Some change completely: child becomes children and mouse becomes mice.

Try it: What is the plural of mouse?

Show answer

Mice.

Prefixes and suffixes

A prefix goes at the beginning of a word and a suffix at the end. Un- means not, so unhappy means not happy.

Try it: What does the word unkind mean?

Show answer

Not kind.
