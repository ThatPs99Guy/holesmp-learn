Math

Congruence and Similarity

Triangle congruence

Triangles are congruent if SSS, SAS, ASA, or AAS holds, or HL for right triangles.

Try it: Two triangles have three pairs of equal sides. Which rule applies?

Show answer

SSS.

Similar triangles

Similar triangles have equal angles and proportional sides. If one triangle has sides 3, 4, 5 and a similar one has sides 6, 8, x, then x = 10.

Try it: Triangles with corresponding sides 2 and 6, and 5 and x, are similar. Find x.

Show answer

15, because the scale factor is 3.

Trigonometry and Circles

Right triangle trigonometry

SOH-CAH-TOA: sin = opposite ÷ hypotenuse, cos = adjacent ÷ hypotenuse, tan = opposite ÷ adjacent.

Try it: In a right triangle with legs 3 and 4 and hypotenuse 5, what is the sine of the angle opposite the side of length 3?

Show answer

3/5.

Circles

Circumference = 2πr and area = πr². For r = 5, C = 10π and A = 25π.

Try it: Find the area of a circle with radius 3.

Show answer

9π.

Geography

Environment and Early Civilizations

River valley civilizations

Early civilizations grew along rivers: the Nile (Egypt), the Tigris and Euphrates (Mesopotamia), the Indus, and the Yellow River (Huang He) in China.

Try it: Which river supported ancient Egypt?

Show answer

The Nile.

Trade routes

The Silk Road connected China with the Mediterranean, moving goods like silk and spices along with ideas and religions.

Try it: What traveled along the Silk Road besides goods?

Show answer

Ideas, religions, and technology.

Climate and Biomes

What shapes climate

Climate is affected by latitude, elevation, nearness to oceans, and ocean currents.

Try it: Why is it usually colder high in the mountains?

Show answer

Temperature drops as elevation increases.

Biomes

A biome is a large region with similar climate and life, such as tropical rain forest, desert, tundra, or temperate forest.

Try it: Which biome is cold and treeless?

Show answer

Tundra.

Language Arts

Reading Informational Text

Rhetorical appeals

Ethos appeals to credibility, pathos to emotion, and logos to logic.

Try it: A speaker cites statistics. Which appeal is that?

Show answer

Logos.

Evaluating sources

A credible source has an expert author, current information, evidence, and few signs of bias. Check who wrote it and why.

Try it: Name one sign that a source is credible.

Show answer

An expert author, cited evidence, or current information.

Writing

Research and synthesis

Synthesis combines ideas from several sources to support your own argument rather than just summarizing each one.

Try it: What is the difference between summary and synthesis?

Show answer

Summary restates one source. Synthesis connects several sources to make a point.

Revising for style

Use parallel structure and vary sentence length. Parallel: She likes hiking, biking, and swimming.

Try it: Make parallel: He likes to run, swim, and biking.

Show answer

He likes to run, swim, and bike.
