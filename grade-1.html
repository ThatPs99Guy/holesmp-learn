Math

Operations and Algebraic Thinking

Word problems within 20

Read the problem, decide whether to add or subtract, and write an equation. Adding joins; subtracting takes away or compares.

Try it: 8 birds sit on a wire and 5 more land. How many birds are there now?

Show answer

13, because 8 + 5 = 13.

Fact families

Three numbers can make four related facts. For 3, 4, and 7: 3 + 4 = 7, 4 + 3 = 7, 7 − 3 = 4, and 7 − 4 = 3.

Try it: Write the fact family for 2, 5, and 7.

Show answer

2 + 5 = 7, 5 + 2 = 7, 7 − 2 = 5, 7 − 5 = 2.

Number and Operations in Base Ten

Tens and ones

A two-digit number is made of tens and ones. 34 is 3 tens and 4 ones.

Try it: What number is 5 tens and 2 ones?

Show answer

52.

Comparing two-digit numbers

Compare the tens first. If the tens match, compare the ones. Use >, <, or =.

Try it: Compare 47 and 52.

Show answer

47 < 52, because 4 tens is less than 5 tens.

Geography

Map and Globe Skills

Cardinal directions

The four main directions are north, south, east, and west. They help us describe where places are.

Try it: What direction is opposite of east?

Show answer

West.

Parts of a map

Most maps have a title, a key, and a compass rose. The title says what the map shows.

Try it: Which part of a map shows the directions?

Show answer

The compass rose.

Georgia and the United States

Where is Georgia?

Georgia is a state in the southeastern United States. Its capital city is Atlanta.

Try it: What is the capital of Georgia?

Show answer

Atlanta.

Landforms and bodies of water

A landform is a shape on Earth's surface, like a mountain, hill, or island. Rivers, lakes, and oceans are bodies of water.

Try it: Is a lake a landform or a body of water?

Show answer

A body of water.

Language Arts

Foundational Skills

Long vowels and silent e

A silent e at the end of a word often makes the vowel say its name: cap becomes cape.

Try it: Add a silent e to kit. What new word do you get?

Show answer

Kite.

Digraphs

Two letters can make one sound: sh, ch, th, and wh. Ship starts with the /sh/ sound.

Try it: Which digraph is in the word chin?

Show answer

ch.

Writing and Language

Writing complete sentences

A sentence starts with a capital letter, tells a whole idea, and ends with punctuation.

Try it: Fix this sentence: the dog runs

Show answer

The dog runs.

Nouns and verbs

A noun names a person, place, or thing. A verb tells what someone or something does.

Try it: In the sentence The cat jumps, which word is the verb?

Show answer

Jumps.
