Math

Expressions and Equations

Slope and linear equations

The equation y = mx + b has slope m and y-intercept b. The line y = 2x + 1 goes through (0, 1) and (1, 3).

Try it: What is the slope of y = −3x + 4?

Show answer

−3.

Solving multi-step equations

Distribute, combine like terms, then use inverse operations. 3(x − 2) = 12 means x − 2 = 4, so x = 6.

Try it: Solve 2(x + 3) = 14.

Show answer

x = 4.

Geometry

The Pythagorean theorem

In a right triangle, a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse. Legs of 6 and 8 give 36 + 64 = 100, so c = 10.

Try it: The legs are 5 and 12. Find the hypotenuse.

Show answer

13.

Transformations

A translation slides, a reflection flips, a rotation turns, and a dilation resizes a figure.

Try it: Reflect the point (2, 3) across the x-axis.

Show answer

(2, −3).

Geography

Georgia's Geography

Regions and features

Georgia's five regions differ in landforms and economy. Brasstown Bald, in the Blue Ridge, is the state's highest point, and the Okefenokee Swamp lies in the south.

Try it: In which region is the Okefenokee Swamp?

Show answer

The Coastal Plain, in south Georgia.

Natural resources

Georgia's resources include pine forests, kaolin clay, and Elberton granite. The fertile Coastal Plain supports many crops.

Try it: Name one natural resource found in Georgia.

Show answer

Pine forests, kaolin, granite, or fertile farmland.

Georgia Government and Economy

Three branches

Georgia's government has three branches: the General Assembly (legislative), the governor (executive), and the courts (judicial).

Try it: Which branch makes Georgia's laws?

Show answer

The legislative branch, the General Assembly.

Agriculture and industry

Georgia is a top producer of peanuts, pecans, and poultry. Atlanta is a major hub for business, transportation, and film.

Try it: Name one crop Georgia is known for.

Show answer

Peanuts or pecans.

Language Arts

Reading Informational Text

Evaluating arguments and evidence

Check whether evidence is relevant and sufficient, and look for flawed reasoning or bias.

Try it: What does it mean for evidence to be sufficient?

Show answer

There is enough of it to support the claim.

Text structure and purpose

Authors organize texts by cause and effect, compare and contrast, problem and solution, or sequence, depending on their purpose.

Try it: Which structure fits an essay about why a bridge collapsed?

Show answer

Cause and effect.

Writing and Language

Argument essays

Introduce the claim, give reasons with evidence, respond to counterclaims, and end with a conclusion that follows from the argument.

Try it: Where does a counterclaim belong in an argument essay?

Show answer

In the body, followed by your response to it.

Verbals and voice

Gerunds (swimming), participles (broken), and infinitives (to run) are verb forms used as other parts of speech. Active voice is usually clearer: The dog chased the ball.

Try it: Change to active voice: The ball was kicked by Sam.

Show answer

Sam kicked the ball.
