Math

Ratios and Proportional Relationships

Proportional relationships

Two quantities are proportional if y = kx, where k is the constant of proportionality. If 3 notebooks cost $6, then k = 2, so 5 notebooks cost $10.

Try it: If 4 pencils cost $3, how much do 12 pencils cost?

Show answer

$9.

Discounts, tax, and tips

A 20% discount takes 20% off the price. For a $40 item, 20% is $8, so the sale price is $32.

Try it: A $50 jacket is 30% off. What is the sale price?

Show answer

$35.

The Number System

Adding and subtracting integers

Use a number line. Subtracting a number is the same as adding its opposite: 4 − (−2) = 4 + 2 = 6.

Try it: What is −3 + 5?

Show answer

2.

Multiplying and dividing integers

Same signs give a positive result. Different signs give a negative result. (−4)(−3) = 12 and (−4)(3) = −12.

Try it: What is (−6) ÷ 2?

Show answer

−3.

Geography

Africa

The Sahara and the Nile

The Sahara is the world's largest hot desert, in northern Africa. The Nile flows north to the Mediterranean Sea and has long supported farming in Egypt.

Try it: Which direction does the Nile flow?

Show answer

North.

Sub-Saharan Africa

South of the Sahara are savannas and rain forests. Mount Kilimanjaro in Tanzania is the highest mountain in Africa.

Try it: Where is Mount Kilimanjaro?

Show answer

In Tanzania, in Africa.

Southwest Asia

Desert and oil

Much of Southwest Asia, also called the Middle East, is dry. The Arabian Peninsula and Persian Gulf region hold some of the world's largest oil reserves.

Try it: Why is oil important to the region's economy?

Show answer

Many countries earn income by selling oil to the world.

Water and rivers

The Tigris and Euphrates Rivers flow through Iraq and nearby countries. Water is scarce in much of the region, so it is very valuable.

Try it: Name one river in Southwest Asia.

Show answer

The Tigris or the Euphrates.

Language Arts

Reading Literature

Theme and summary

A theme develops over a whole text. A good summary states the main ideas without your personal opinion.

Try it: Should a summary include your personal opinion?

Show answer

No.

Setting, plot, and point of view

The setting can shape the plot, and the narrator's point of view affects what the reader learns.

Try it: How does a first-person narrator limit what we know?

Show answer

We only learn what that narrator sees, thinks, and feels.

Language

Phrases and clauses

A clause has a subject and a verb. An independent clause can stand alone. A dependent clause cannot: because it rained.

Try it: Is because it rained a complete sentence?

Show answer

No. It is a dependent clause.

Commas, semicolons, and colons

Use a semicolon to join two related independent clauses: I studied; I passed. Use a colon before a list or explanation.

Try it: Which punctuation mark joins two related independent clauses without a conjunction?

Show answer

A semicolon.
