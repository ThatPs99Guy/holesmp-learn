Math

Precalculus: Functions and Trigonometry

Radians and the unit circle

180° equals π radians. To convert degrees to radians, multiply by π/180. 90° = π/2.

Try it: Convert 60° to radians.

Show answer

π/3.

Composition of functions

f(g(x)) means put g(x) inside f. If f(x) = 2x + 1 and g(x) = x², then f(g(3)) = 2(9) + 1 = 19.

Try it: Using the same functions, find g(f(1)).

Show answer

9, because f(1) = 3 and g(3) = 9.

Statistics and Probability

Measures of center and spread

The mean is the average and the median is the middle value. Standard deviation measures how spread out data are. For 2, 4, 6 the mean is 4.

Try it: Find the mean of 3, 5, 10.

Show answer

6.

Independent events

For independent events, P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B). A coin landing heads and a die showing 6: 1/2 × 1/6 = 1/12.

Try it: What is the probability of heads on a coin and a 3 on a die?

Show answer

1/12.

Geography

Government and Geography

The Electoral College

Each state has electors equal to its House seats plus its two senators. There are 538 electors in all (including 3 for the District of Columbia), and a candidate needs 270 to win.

Try it: How many electoral votes does a candidate need to win?

Show answer

270.

Census and apportionment

A census counts the U.S. population every ten years. The 435 House seats are reassigned among the states based on the results.

Try it: How many seats are in the U.S. House of Representatives?

Show answer

435.

Global Connections

Trade and globalization

Globalization connects economies through trade. Imports come into a country and exports leave it.

Try it: Is a car made in Japan and sold in the U.S. an import or an export for the U.S.?

Show answer

An import.

Resources and sustainability

Renewable resources, like solar and wind, replenish naturally. Nonrenewable resources, like coal and oil, do not on a human timescale.

Try it: Is natural gas renewable or nonrenewable?

Show answer

Nonrenewable.

Language Arts

British and World Literature

Satire and irony

Satire uses humor or exaggeration to criticize. Jonathan Swift's A Modest Proposal (1729) is a famous satire that uses an absurd suggestion to criticize attitudes toward the poor.

Try it: What is the purpose of satire?

Show answer

To criticize people or society using humor, irony, or exaggeration.

Comparing themes across texts

Choose a theme, such as power or identity, and show how two texts treat it differently, with evidence from each.

Try it: What should you include when comparing two texts?

Show answer

A shared theme and evidence from both texts.

Writing and Communication

College-ready essays

A strong personal essay uses a specific story, a clear reflection, and an organized structure with a clear voice.

Try it: What makes a personal essay stronger than a list of achievements?

Show answer

A specific story with reflection on what it shows.

Presentations

Speak clearly, make eye contact, use evidence, and prepare for questions. Visuals should support your points, not repeat everything.

Try it: Name one habit of an effective presenter.

Show answer

Eye contact, clear speaking, or evidence-based points.
