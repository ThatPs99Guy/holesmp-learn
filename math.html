1. Fractions

Adding and subtracting

Fractions need a common denominator (the same bottom number) before you add or subtract. Change the fractions, then work with the tops only.

Example: 1/2 + 1/3
Common denominator is 6: 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6

Multiplying

Multiply the tops, multiply the bottoms, then simplify.

Example: 2/3 × 3/4 = 6/12 = 1/2

Dividing

Keep the first fraction, flip the second (use its reciprocal), and multiply.

Example: 1/2 ÷ 1/4 = 1/2 × 4/1 = 4/2 = 2

2. Percents

"Percent" means "out of 100." To find a percent of a number, change the percent to a decimal and multiply.

Example: What is 25% of 80?
25% = 0.25, and 0.25 × 80 = 20

Percent change

percent change = (new − old) ÷ old × 100

Example: A price goes from $50 to $60.
(60 − 50) ÷ 50 = 0.2, so it is a 20% increase.

3. Order of operations

Work in this order, left to right within each step:

  1. Parentheses
  2. Exponents
  3. Multiplication and Division (left to right)
  4. Addition and Subtraction (left to right)
Example: 3 + 4 × 2
Multiply first: 3 + 8 = 11

With parentheses: (3 + 4) × 2 = 7 × 2 = 14

4. Area and perimeter

ShapeAreaPerimeter / circumference
Rectanglelength × width2 × (length + width)
Triangle1/2 × base × heightadd the three sides
Circleπ × r²2 × π × r
Example: A rectangle is 5 by 3.
Area = 5 × 3 = 15 square units. Perimeter = 2 × (5 + 3) = 16 units.

Area is measured in square units, and perimeter in plain units. That is a common thing to mix up.

5. Solving equations

Whatever you do to one side of an equation, do to the other side. Undo operations in reverse order: first add or subtract, then multiply or divide.

Example: 2x + 5 = 17
Subtract 5 from both sides: 2x = 12
Divide both sides by 2: x = 6
Check: 2(6) + 5 = 17 ✓
